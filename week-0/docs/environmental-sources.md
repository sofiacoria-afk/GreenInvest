# GREENInvest environmental data sources

GREENInvest will use free sources only.

## 1. Official company annual / sustainability reports
Primary source for the number displayed to users. Store the reporting year, Scope 1, Scope 2, unit and exact source URL. Never invent missing values.

## 2. Bolsa Mexicana de Valores sustainability disclosures
Use BMV IFRS S1/S2 disclosures as a second source when available to cross-check company-reported emissions and methodology.

## 3. Climate TRACE
Use as an independent reference. Do not combine Climate TRACE estimates directly with company-reported Scope 1/2 values because organizational boundaries and methodology can differ.

## Comparison rule
GREENInvest should prefer showing the company's change over time instead of ranking different industries only by absolute emissions.

Suggested trend labels:
- Green: emissions decreasing by more than 2%
- Yellow: change between -2% and +2%
- Red: emissions increasing by more than 2%

The source and reporting year must always be visible next to environmental results.
